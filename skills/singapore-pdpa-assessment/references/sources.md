# Sources and versions

This skill indexes its sources; it does not reproduce them. Retrieve the text of every
provision and guideline passage you rely on, for the law-as-at date, in each assessment.

## The Act

**Personal Data Protection Act 2012**, on [Singapore Statutes Online](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012).
The checklist in `obligations.json` follows the table of contents of the version shown as
"Current version as at 20 Oct 2026", recorded from a user-saved browser copy. That copy's
version timeline showed, among others:

| Version from | Amended by |
|---|---|
| 16 Oct 2026 (current at recording) | Act 2 of 2026 |
| 5 Dec 2025 | Act 19 of 2025 |
| 1 Oct 2022 | Act 40 of 2020 |
| 1 Apr 2022 | Act 25 of 2021 |
| 31 Dec 2021 | 2020 Revised Edition |
| 1 Feb 2021 | Act 40 of 2020 |

The recording did not examine what Act 19 of 2025 or Act 2 of 2026 changed. Before relying on
any provision, check the version in force on the law-as-at date and whether these amendments
touched it. The same table of contents lists no Part 6B, although section 4 refers to Parts 6,
6A and 6B; establish its status rather than assuming either way.

Retrieve provisions with the `singapore-legal-research` skill's statutory evidence procedure
and its access rules, or from a copy the user supplies. Section numbers in this skill are a map,
not a substitute for the text.

## PDPC guidance

**Advisory Guidelines on Key Concepts in the PDPA**, issued 23 September 2013, revised
29 April 2026, published by the [Personal Data Protection Commission](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/organisations/regulations-decisions/regulatory-guidance/advisory-guidelines-on-key-concepts-in-the-personal-data-protection-act).
Page references in `obligations.json` are to that revision. Guidelines are not law: they show
how the Commission interprets the Act, and they may predate a later amendment. Do Not Call
obligations are covered by separate PDPC guidance.

Where enforcement practice matters, PDPC's published decisions show how obligations are
applied. Cite a decision only after reading it.
